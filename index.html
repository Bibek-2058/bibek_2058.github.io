How to Convert BS to AD Dates: A Complete Guide

July 30, 2026  |  5 min read

The Bikram Sambat (BS) calendar is the official calendar of Nepal, used for all legal, cultural, and religious purposes. Converting between BS and AD can be confusing due to the 56.7-year difference and the varying month lengths. This guide explains everything you need to know about BS-AD conversion.

The BS calendar is approximately 56 years and 8 months ahead of the Gregorian (AD) calendar. For example, BS 2080 corresponds to AD 2023-2024. The conversion is not simply adding 56 or 57 years because the BS calendar has a different number of days in each month and a different New Year date (typically around April 13-14).

Nepali Land Measurement Units: Ropani, Aana, Paisa Explained

July 25, 2026  |  6 min read

Nepal has a traditional system of land measurement that is still widely used today, especially in real estate, agriculture, and land surveying. The main units are Ropani, Aana, Paisa, and Daam. Understanding these units is essential for anyone dealing with property in Nepal.

The system works as follows: 1 Ropani = 16 Aana, 1 Aana = 4 Paisa, and 1 Paisa = 4 Daam. In terms of square feet, 1 Ropani is approximately 5,476 sq. ft. (or 508.74 sq. meters). Each Aana is about 342.25 sq. ft., each Paisa is about 85.56 sq. ft., and each Daam is about 21.39 sq. ft.

These units are officially recognized in Nepal and are used in property deeds, government records, and land surveys. Our Area Converter supports these units, making it easy to convert between Nepali and international measurement systems.

  • Ropani – Primary land measurement unit in Nepal
  • Aana – 1/16 of a Ropani
  • Paisa – 1/4 of an Aana
  • Daam – 1/4 of a Paisa (smallest unit)
  • Used for property transactions, land registration, and farming
