About Study Assist

Welcome to Study Assist, a dedicated educational platform designed to empower students, civil service aspirants, and learners across Nepal and beyond.

Our primary mission is to simplify learning by delivering high-quality study materials, comprehensive Public Service Commission (PSC / Lok Sewa) syllabus notes, specialized technical topics, and insightful educational blogs. Whether you are preparing for competitive examinations or looking to expand your knowledge base, Study Assist offers curated, structured content to support your academic and career journey.

Founded and managed by Study Assist Team, Study Assist is committed to continuous improvement, accessibility, and excellence in educational resource sharing.
